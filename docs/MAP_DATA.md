# Campus map data

## Source and method

The map was traced from the seven supplied screenshots: one overview plus closeups
of the northern entrance, academic blocks, sports/MBA area, western institutes and
southern residential/school area. Shared landmarks were used to align the different
zoom levels. The original screenshots are reference material and are not bundled
inside the Android app.

## Included labels

The 34 choices include UVPCE New and Main, MBA/VMPIM New, Nescafe, University
Office, Pharmacy, AMPICS, ICT, Institute of Technology, Maritime Studies, VM Patel
College of Management, MUIS/Computer Science, KKIASR, the main gate, banks, sports
facilities, Auditorium, JIM, G-Block Dining, Uma Hostel, both labelled schools,
Shopping Centre, Executive Residences, KVK, Kitchen Garden and Ayurvedic Hospital.

Unreadable/truncated labels were not guessed. Pearson VUE and fotonVR sit in blocks
already represented by MBA/VMPIM New and UVPCE New/Maritime respectively.

## Important approximation

The screenshot does not literally label “UVPCE New Building.” Its app marker is on
the large southeast new-engineering block carrying Maritime Studies/fotonVR labels.
If a campus visit confirms a different entrance, edit the `uvpce_new` place's node
in `app/src/main/assets/campus_map.json`.

The edges follow visible campus roads and obvious short access paths. They do not
claim surveyed walking distances, accessibility details or live closures.
